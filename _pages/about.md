I am an assistant professor at the [Center for Tracking and Society](https://cts.ku.dk/), Department of Communication, University of Copenhagen. Previously I worked on the [Power over Platforms?](https://cts.ku.dk/projects/power-over-platforms/?obvius_session_id=012b79b028990cb2b72a557abba11897) project as a postdoctoral researcher, and completed my PhD in Information, Communication, and the Social Sciences at the [Oxford Internet Institute](https://www.oii.ox.ac.uk/people/profiles/diyi-liu/), University of Oxford (2021-25), which was generously funded by the Clarendon Scholarship. I earned my BA and MA in International Journalism and Communication in China prior to commencing my PhD.

My research examines the legitimacy of platformized and AI-augmented communication from a comparative, human-centered perspective. I study how digital platforms shape public discourse at the institutional level and how people experience and engage with increasingly AI-augmented systems in everyday life. By mobilizing on-the-ground user insights, my work advances empirically grounded normative debates about legitimacy and accountability in platform and AI governance.

My latest work has appeared in peer-reviewed journals such as [Social Media + Society](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/20563051251340855), [Policy & Internet](https://doi.org/10.1002/poi3.388), [New Media & Society](https://doi.org/10.1177/14614448241259149), [Journal of Medical Internet Research](https://doi.org/10.2196/54135), and media outlets such as [The Diplomat](https://thediplomat.com/2019/09/bridging-the-gaps-in-the-asia-pacific-information-superhighway/). Previously, I interned with UNESCO’s Communication and Information Unit. Starting from 2024 I have also been engaged with Meta's Actor and Behavior Policy Expert Circle. I hold visiting and fellowship positions at several institutions: a visiting PhD researcher at the Department of Communications and New Media at the National University of Singapore (Fall 2023), an Open Research Fellow at the Weizenbaum Institute in Germany (Spring 2024), a research fellow at the Centre for Strategic and International Studies (CSIS) in Indonesia (Fall 2024), and a Tech Policy Fellow at the Tech Global Institute (2025).
